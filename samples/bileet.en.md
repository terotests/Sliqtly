# Party! {fx=spectrum fx-dark=0.35 fx-x=0.78}

Press **Present**: the music starts and the slides move with it.

**M** turns the music off and on.

# Kaleidoscope {fx=kaleido}

The bass turns the pattern, and every beat flashes from the middle.

# Ridges {fx=ridges fx-dark=0.4}

A second and a half of the spectrum, the newest row in front.

# Make your own

Add an mp3 to the files and name it at the top:

```yaml
---
music: media/song.mp3
---
```

A slide's effect: `{fx=spectrum}`, `{fx=kaleido}` or `{fx=ridges}`, with settings like `fx-hue=200` or `fx-dark=0.5`.
