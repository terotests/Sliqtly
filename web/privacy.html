We do not sell personal data, show advertising or build profiles. We do not use your content to train AI models. Under Google Cloud's terms, Vertex AI does not use it for training either.

Cookies and browser storage

The editor uses one cookie, __session, which carries your sign-in for at most an hour at a time. The site keeps settings such as language, look and the length of a link in your browser's local storage; they never leave your browser. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

Presentations you share by link can be opened by anyone who has the link. When an AI assistant (such as Claude or ChatGPT) works with Sliqtly through MCP, what you write there is processed by that assistant's provider under your agreement with them, not by us.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest, and receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable form. Most of this you can do yourself: presentations can be exported and deleted in the editor, and File → Published in the cloud lists what you have shared. For the rest, write to the contact address above; we answer within a month.

You may also complain to the Finnish data protection authority, the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman, or to the authority where you live.

Security

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Presentations are private or shared by link as you choose; private ones can be read only by their owner. Sign-in tokens are stored as hashes. No system is perfectly secure: do not put data in Sliqtly that would cause serious harm if disclosed, such as health data or secrets.

Children

Sliqtly is not meant for children under 13, and they should not create an account.

Changes

We will update this policy when what we process changes. Material changes are announced on this page and by email to account holders before they take effect.
